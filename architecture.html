State transition flow inside CourseOffering

1 · Lock and validateAcquire the per-offering lock; look up learner ID; return a clear invalid or duplicate status when applicable.
2 · Check prerequisiteReject an ineligible new request. Existing enrolled/waitlisted duplicates are reported before rechecking eligibility.
3 · Enroll or queueWith a seat, add to enrolled. If full, append the eligible learner once to the FIFO waitlist.
4 · Drop and promoteRemove the enrolled ID, scan the queue, retain ineligible entries, promote the earliest eligible one, then unlock.

State is local to one CourseOffering object and exists only in memory. A dropped seat is filled immediately by the first eligible learner found in the FIFO scan.

Design rules and invariants

A single, explicit eligibility policy avoids introducing a configurable policy engine.

  • ✓Enrollment: unknown IDs are invalid; duplicate enrolled/waitlisted IDs are reported without duplication; prerequisite failure is rejected. Eligible learners take available seats, otherwise they join the queue.
  • ✓Promotion: after a successful drop, scan the queue from the head and promote the first learner eligible at scan time.
  • ✓Ineligible queue entries: skip without removing; preserve relative position for a later seat opening. They are not promoted until eligible.
  • ✓Capacity: enrolled count remains between zero and capacity; a promoted learner fills exactly one newly freed seat.
  • ✓Safety: lock serializes operations on one offering; constructor snapshots learner-map structure and verifies map keys match learner IDs; read APIs return immutable copies.
  • ✓Clear outcomes: status enums and result records distinguish enrollment, waitlisting, invalid IDs, repeated requests, successful drops, and promotion results.

Limitations and follow-up risks

Scope is intentionally limited to one offering's enrollment/drop/promotion slice.

  • !State is in-memory and not shared across instances or processes; there is no persistence or recovery.
  • !Prerequisite is a boolean flag, not a course-level prerequisite model. Learner IDs and names are non-null but are not normalized or required to be non-blank.
  • !Map structure is copied, but Learner objects remain mutable. Eligibility changes do not trigger promotion until a successful drop opens a seat.
  • !No frontend, CLI, catalog, payments, identity integration, multi-term registration, deployment, or database is provided.
  • !Locking protects only this object in this process; it is not a distributed coordination mechanism.

Human-reviewed policy choices

AI suggestions were treated as a draft. The documented rule is: reject ineligible learners at enrollment; when promoting, skip ineligible queued learners but retain their position. The implementation and tests reflect this stated assumption.

No AI model, API, credentials, or external AI service is called at runtime. Prompt summaries, decisions, and verification evidence are recorded in AI-PROMPT-LOG.md.
